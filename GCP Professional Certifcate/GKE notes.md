Google Kubernetes Engine, or GKE, is a powerful container orchestration tool that helps you manage your clusters in Google Cloud.

With fleets, you can view and control clusters from a single view in the Google Cloud Console.
But fleets are more than just groups of clusters.

Within a fleet of clusters, some Kubernetes objects that have the same name, like namespaces, are treated equally, even if they are in different clusters.
This is called sameness, and it simplifies fleet management.
